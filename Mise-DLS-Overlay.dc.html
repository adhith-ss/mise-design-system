Alert Dialog

A confirmation for something irreversible or expensive. The title asks the question, the body states the consequence, and the confirm button names the action.

Cancel PO-4471? Harbor Produce has already confirmed this order. Cancelling after 8:00 PM may still be charged.
Send PO-4471 to Harbor Produce? $1,486.10 · delivery Tue 6:00 AM. You can undo for 10 minutes after sending.
Props & accessibility title · description · confirmLabel — a verb, never "OK" · cancelLabel · tone 'default' | 'danger' · onConfirm · loading · requireTypedConfirmation string, for deletions that cannot be undone. Role alertdialog; focus lands on the cancel button for danger tone, Escape cancels, and there is no backdrop dismissal.

Popover

A small piece of interactive content anchored to its trigger — a filter panel, a column picker, a quick edit. It does not block the page.

Filter invoices
Rules Opens on click, never on hover. Closes on outside click, Escape, or Apply. Positioning flips near viewport edges through usePopover. A Popover never contains another Popover; nested choices become a Dialog.
Props & accessibility trigger · open / defaultOpen / onOpenChange · placement · offset number, 6 · width number | 'trigger' · arrow boolean, false · closeOnInteractOutside boolean, true · portal boolean, true. Trigger sets aria-expanded and aria-controls; focus moves in on open and returns to the trigger on close, but the rest of the page stays operable.

Tooltip

A short label for a control whose meaning is not fully visible. Never holds information the operator needs to complete a task, and never holds a control.

Filter rows
Rules 300ms open delay, no close delay, one tooltip at a time. Appears on hover and keyboard focus. Maximum two lines; if it needs more, the interface needs a hint under the field.
Props & accessibility content string · placement · delay ms, 300 · disabled · maxWidth number, 240. Role tooltip referenced by aria-describedby — or used as the control's aria-label when the trigger is icon-only. Escape dismisses without moving focus, and it never covers its own trigger.
