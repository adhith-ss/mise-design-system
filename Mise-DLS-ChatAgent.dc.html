Chat & Agent

The surface where Mise's agent works alongside an operator: ordering, reconciling invoices, chasing credits. The agent may read anything and draft anything, but money leaves the restaurant only after a person approves it. These components exist to make what the agent did, and what it is about to do, legible at a glance.

Message Bubble

One turn in the conversation. Operator turns are tinted and right-aligned; agent turns sit flush left on the page surface with no bubble, so long answers read as documents rather than chat.

Variants

Did Harbor short us on romaine again this week?
Yes — twice. Tuesday's delivery was 4 cases short against PO-4471, and Thursday's was 2 short. Both were invoiced in full, so you are owed $138.00. Checked 2 invoices · 1.4s
Drafting the credit request now

Do Stream tokens into their final layout, so nothing reflows when the turn completes. Keep the footer line — sources touched and elapsed time — on every agent turn that read data.
Don't Don't animate a typing indicator in place of first tokens, and don't bubble agent turns — a bubble caps line length and makes tables and tool cards unreadable.

Tool-call Card

A record of one thing the agent did to the system: a query, a lookup, a draft. Collapsed it states the action and its result in one line; expanded it shows the arguments and the returned rows.

Props tool string · summary — the human sentence, required · args object · result ReactNode | rows · status 'running' | 'done' | 'error' | 'denied' · duration ms · defaultExpanded boolean, false · onRetry · writesData boolean — a write shows the amber dot and links its approval.
Rules & accessibility Collapsed by default, and the summary alone must be enough to trust or challenge the turn. Never hide a write behind a collapsed card. The header is a button with aria-expanded; running state uses aria-busy, not a spinner announcement.

Inline Approval

The gate between a draft and a commitment. It states exactly what will happen, to whom, and for how much, and it is the only component in the system that turns agent intent into an action against a vendor.

Needs your approval expires in 12 min
Send credit request for $138.00 to Harbor Produce
{{ f.label }} {{ f.value }}
Drafted from 2 short deliveries against PO-4471. The message quotes both invoice numbers and the received counts.
Approved by Dana R. · 8:14 AM · undo for 10 min
Declined · agent will not retry without being asked
Do Put the amount, the counterparty, and the irreversibility in the title. Offer Edit draft next to Approve so the operator can correct rather than abandon.
Don't Don't label the primary "Confirm", don't pre-select it for keyboard Enter, and never let an approval scroll out of the transcript unanswered — pin it until it is resolved.
Props & accessibility title · facts label/value pairs, 2–6 · rationale · impact 'reversible' | 'undoable' | 'permanent' · expiresAt · onApprove / onEdit / onDecline · approver — resolved state stamp · requiresRole string. Rendered as a region labelled by its title, announced assertively when it appears; focus is not stolen, but the composer shows a pending-approval affordance. Approve is never the default focus.

Agent Status

What the agent is doing right now, in the operator's words. Named steps replace indeterminate spinners for any task over two seconds, and every step can be interrupted.

Reading invoices · 3 of 8
Waiting on your approval

Props & accessibility state 'idle' | 'working' | 'waiting' | 'stopped' · steps with label, status, meta · current index · onStop — always present while working · compact boolean for the header pill. Announced politely on step change, at most once every two seconds; the pill's meaning is carried in text as well as its dot colour.

Citation

A pointer from a claim to the record it came from — an invoice line, a PO, a vendor agreement. Every number the agent states about money carries one.

Props & accessibility label — the record's real identifier, never a bare number · recordType 'invoice' | 'order' | 'item' | 'vendor' | 'policy' · href · preview fields shown in the hover card · confidence 'exact' | 'inferred' — inferred renders in ink outline, not brand green. A link, focusable in reading order, with an accessible name that includes the record type. The preview is never the only path to the record.

Suggestion Chip

Two to four next moves offered after a turn. Each is a real action the agent can take, phrased as the operator would say it.

States:
Rules, props & accessibility Maximum four, one line each, never wrapping mid-phrase. A chip may draft but never commits — anything that spends money routes to Inline Approval. items with label and intent · onSelect · max number, 4 · dismissible boolean. Rendered as a labelled group of buttons after the turn, so screen-reader users meet the answer first; chips disappear once the operator types.

Composer

Where the operator writes. It grows to six lines, accepts invoice photos, and shows the scope the agent is working in so an instruction is never applied to the wrong location.

Ask about orders, invoices, or vendors…
Downtown kitchen
One approval is waiting — answer it before sending a new instruction. Review
Props & accessibility value / onChange · onSend · scope — the location or period the agent acts on · attachments · maxRows number, 6 · disabled · pendingApproval boolean · placeholder. Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line, and Escape returns focus to the transcript. A labelled textarea, never a contenteditable div; the scope chip is part of the field's accessible description.

Agent Error

When the agent cannot finish. It says what stopped, what it did complete, and what the operator can do — including doing it by hand.

{{ e.title }} {{ e.body }}
Do Keep the partial work visible and say plainly whether anything was sent. Always offer the manual route as the second action.
Don't Don't surface provider names, status codes, or stack traces, and don't apologise twice. Never auto-retry a write.
Props & accessibility kind 'tool' | 'permission' | 'timeout' | 'refusal' | 'network' · title · body · completed — what did land · onRetry (reads only) · manualHref · reportId for support. Announced assertively once; the retry button is focusable but not focused, and the error stays in the transcript as part of the record.
