Navigation

Mise has two axes: which unit you are looking at, and which part of the back office. The unit lives in the Top Nav and persists; sections live in the Side Nav. Nothing else competes at that level.

Top Nav

The app's fixed header: product mark, the unit switcher, global search, and account. It never carries page-level actions.

Accessibility A banner landmark holding a nav labelled "Global". Skip-to-content is the first tab stop. The unit switcher announces the current unit as part of its name, because getting the wrong unit is the most expensive mistake in the product.

Side Nav

Sections of the back office, grouped and always visible. Counts appear only where they change what the operator does next.
