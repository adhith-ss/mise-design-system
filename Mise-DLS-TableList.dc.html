Tree List

Nested records — a recipe's sub-recipes, a category hierarchy, a chart of accounts. Two or three levels; deeper than that is a search problem, not a tree.

Role tree with treeitem children, aria-expanded and aria-level; right and left arrows expand and collapse, up and down move, and useTreeFocus keeps one tab stop.

Overflow List

A horizontal set that measures itself and moves what does not fit into a "+n" menu. Used for filter chips, vendor tags, and toolbars in narrow panels.

Props & accessibility items · renderItem · minVisible number, 1 · overflowLabel (count) => string · placement 'end' | 'start' · gap number, 8.
