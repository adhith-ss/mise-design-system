# Chat & Agent

The surface where Mise's agent works alongside an operator: ordering,
reconciling invoices, chasing credits. The agent may read anything and draft
anything, but money leaves the restaurant only after a person approves it. These
components exist to make what the agent did, and what it is about to do,
legible at a glance.

## Principles

**Read freely, write on approval.** The agent can query any record and draft any
document. Sending an order, a credit request, or a payment always passes through
`InlineApproval`.

**Show the work, not the machinery.** Every claim links to its record; every
action leaves a tool-call card. No prompts, model names, or token counts in the
interface.

**Named steps over spinners.** Anything over two seconds reports what it is
doing in the operator's vocabulary — reading invoices, matching lines — and can
be stopped.

**The transcript is a record.** Approvals, declines, and errors stay in place
with their timestamps and approver. Nothing is quietly removed once shown.

## What the agent is about to do

The only component that turns agent intent into an action against a vendor. The

allowed to scroll away, and the composer blocks new instructions until it is
resolved.

## Composition

A full agent panel is: `AgentStatus` (compact) in the header, a transcript of
`Message` turns with `ToolCallCard` and `Citation` inside them,
`InlineApproval` pinned when pending, `SuggestionChips` after the last turn,
and `Composer` at the foot.
