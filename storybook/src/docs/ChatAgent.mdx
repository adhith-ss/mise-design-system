# Chat & Agent

Plato helps operators inspect menu margins, prepare weekly reviews and compare forecast scenarios. The operator remains the decision-maker. Show data freshness and evidence before a recommendation; show what will change before asking for approval.

## Principles

**Read freely, write on approval.** The agent can query any record and draft any
document within the user's granted permissions. Sending an order, a credit request, or a payment always passes through
`InlineApproval`.

**Show the work, not the machinery.** Every claim links to its record; every
action leaves a tool-call card. No prompts, model names, or token counts in the
interface.

**Named steps over spinners.** Anything over two seconds reports what it is
doing in the operator's vocabulary — reading invoices, matching lines — and can
be stopped.

**The transcript is a record.** Approvals, declines, and errors stay in place
with their timestamps and approver. Nothing is quietly removed once shown.

## What the agent is about to do

<Canvas of={ApprovalStories.Pending} />

The only component that turns agent intent into an action against a vendor. The
title names the amount and the counterparty; the facts grid is enough to decide
from without opening anything.

Approve is never the default focus. Keep unanswered work available without blocking unrelated questions. Require a reason when the operator changes a target plate cost.

## Context and continuity

Suggestions follow the current page. A generated brief appears as a collapsed, timestamped item above suggestions on subsequent visits; it must not replace the conversation. Let the operator dismiss it or continue another task. Persistent cross-session history, live source freshness and write approvals are integration requirements, not capabilities to imply before a backend exists.

## Composition

A full agent panel is: `AgentStatus` (compact) in the header, a transcript of
`Message` turns with `ToolCallCard` and `Citation` inside them,
`InlineApproval` pinned when pending, `SuggestionChips` after the last turn,
and `Composer` at the foot.
